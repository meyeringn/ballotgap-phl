Voting rights and accommodations

Accessible voting systems

Pennsylvania polling places must provide an accessible voting system. Ask poll workers about accessible voting and assistance.

Assistance of your choice

Voters who need assistance because of a disability may choose someone to help, with legal restrictions on certain people.

Alternative ballot and other options

Philadelphia describes alternative ballot options for eligible voters whose assigned locations do not have the FH designation. Rules and deadlines matter. Confirm your eligibility and instructions with the City Commissioners.
