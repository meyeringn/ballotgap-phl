About BallotGap & our methods

Built for voters and advocates

BallotGap PHL is an independent, nonpartisan exploration of published Philadelphia polling-place accessibility classifications. It is not operated or endorsed by the City Commissioners, REV UP, or Committee of Seventy.

Philadelphia Atlas · City Commissioners · Committee of Seventy · REV UP

How to interpret the data

The source is the public City Commissioners polling-place dataset served through CARTO. One record represents a ward/division entry. FH means building F and parking H, not a comprehensive accessibility audit. Unique buildings are estimated from normalized addresses, which may combine or split locations. Council districts depend on a separate GIS lookup. The map, transit stop estimates, and provisional division lookup are not substitutes for official voter assignments.
