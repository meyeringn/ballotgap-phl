Your Rights as a Disabled Voter in Philadelphia

Accessible Voting MachinesEvery polling place in Pennsylvania must have at least one accessible voting system. You can ask poll workers for help using it.
Bring Someone to HelpIf you need help voting due to a disability, you can bring a person of your choice to assist you in the voting booth (with some restrictions on who).
Alternative Ballot OptionsIf your polling place isn't fully accessible, you can vote by mail, request an Alternative Ballot, or vote at City Hall Room 142 on Election Day. Contact (215) 686-VOTE or visit vote.phila.gov.
